## Supplementary references and corrections

Use the Sources tab for clickable references and contextual clarifications. These include ASHA evidence-based practice, open Research Methods in Psychology, Clinical Research Methods in Speech-Language Pathology and Audiology (publisher sample), Belmont principles, original SPIDER and ECLIPSE papers, JBI, PRISMA, PubMed guidance, Cochrane, NLM and Zotero documentation.

Clarifications distinguish random sampling from allocation, mediator from moderator, reliability from validity and responsiveness, probability sampling from guaranteed representativeness, and statistical from clinical significance. Decibels are logarithmic, a p value is not the probability that the null is true, and n=30 does not make raw observations normal. Gain scores do not remove every confound; ANCOVA requires design-appropriate assumptions rather than a blanket prohibition in nonrandomized studies. The provided tutorial contains artificial examples rather than clinical-trial efficacy results.
